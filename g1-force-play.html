G1: reference and estimated forces in the stock play scene

Both runs use IsaacContrib-Velocity-Sand-G1-29dof-MPM-Play, the same policy checkpoint, identical recorded initial physical states and observations, and identical saved task configurations. Their native initial rendered frames also match pixel for pixel. The robot, material bed, retaining geometry, approach platform, and saved native camera settings are preserved.

On the left, MPM contact forces drive G1. On the right, the analytical contact estimate replaces material feedback. MuJoCo-Warp advances the actuated robot in both runs, and MPM computes particle motion in both runs. The estimate uses 200 solver iterations and effective particle mass multiplier 100.

Measured outcomes

Both runs stop at the task's native workspace boundary, after 3.68 s with reference feedback and 4.04 s with estimated feedback. Neither reaches the requested full duration; neither records a bad-orientation termination. During material contact, the per-foot force-vector RMSE is 97.18 N on the reference-driven trajectory and 158.52 N on the estimate-driven trajectory. These substantial errors do not establish robust force reconstruction.
