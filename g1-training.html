G1 particle NeRD evaluation

22 October 2026. Twenty seconds requested per evaluation. Dynamic simulations use effective material mass multiplier 50, 200 solver iterations and relaxation 0.02. Particle model: validation-selected update 900, trained with a 0.5-second recurrent horizon.

Native G1 MPM newton_rtx settings: 1920 × 1080 per simulation view, read directly from the task configuration with no resolution override. Task-authored materials, particle widths, sky lighting and robot-tracking camera are retained. Two views plus plots form a 3840 × 1400 video. Use the full-resolution links or fullscreen for inspection.

Evaluation geometry: 3 × 3 × 0.3 m sand, 88,360 particles, 0.04 m MPM grid. Physics/prediction: 200 Hz; policy: 50 Hz. New reference recordings are evaluation-only; no model training or training dataset collection was performed.
