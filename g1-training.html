G1 particle NeRD evaluation

22 October 2026. Evaluation of the validation-selected checkpoint (update 900) after training with a 0.5-second recurrent horizon.

All videos use the native newton_rtx visualizer and the existing G1 MPM task’s authored robot and testbed materials, sand color and particle widths, sky lighting, and robot-tracking camera. Recorded evaluation geometry is retained: 3 × 3 × 0.3 m sand, 88,360 particles, 0.04 m MPM grid. Physics and particle predictions run at 200 Hz; the robot policy runs at 50 Hz.
