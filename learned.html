Implementation

  1. Predict every particle's next position with a graph neural network conditioned on material properties and rigid geometry.
  2. Compute rigid-body forces with the analytical contact estimator and feed the resulting body motion back into the particle predictor.
  3. Train and evaluate on separate episodes spanning granular material parameters, body mass, and box/sphere/cylinder geometry.
  4. Compare analytic distance queries with sampled signed-distance fields (SDFs).
