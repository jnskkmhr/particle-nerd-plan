In progress

Correction to data collection

The original collector prescribed the box trajectory. This was inconsistent with the intended experiment. The revised collector gives a 2 kg box a random initial position, orientation, and velocity, then advances it with MJWarp coupled to MPM sand. It does not command the box after reset.

For a freely moving box, the measured change in linear momentum should equal gravity plus all applied contact impulses. With lagged coupling, the MPM reaction computed in one step is applied in the next rigid-body step. Both quantities are recorded so the comparison uses matching intervals. Any direct rigid-ground or rigid-wall contacts must also be included.

Dynamic reference validation

Four 400-step episodes completed with 500 material particles per episode. The mean box force-balance residual is 0.000002 N. The ground and walls remained fixed. The sand reaction at the end of the episodes was 19.3–20.6 N upward, close to the 2 kg box’s weight of 19.62 N. No direct rigid contact occurred.

This checks the reference simulation, not force prediction. The original force estimator is being evaluated separately on the dynamic trajectories.
