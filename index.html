05 / Particle Transolver: Slice Attention & Displacement

Physics-attention slices aggregate and distribute particle tokens

Quadrature-weighted aggregation into S = 64 physics slices

Aᵢₛ = softmaxs(Wslice gᵢ / τ)   ·   zₛ = (Σᵢ mᵢ Aᵢₛ vᵢ) / (Σᵢ mᵢ Aᵢₛ + 0.01)

Each token i carries quadrature multiplicity mᵢ (mᵢ = 1 for full bed or active particles; mᵢ = (N − K)/Mvalid for background anchors), keeping slice summaries invariant to active-subset compaction.

All-to-all attention in 64-slice token space

z′ = Attention(Q(z), K(z), V(z))   ·   h′ᵢ = hᵢ + Wout(Σₛ Aᵢₛ z′ₛ)

Each block applies LayerNorm, PhysicsAttention, and a 4× GELU MLP with residual connections. No spatial neighbor graph is constructed during neural evaluation.

Direct 3D displacement prediction

Δxᵢ = sdisp · Decoder(hᵢ(L))   ·   x̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ = xᵢⁿ + Δxᵢ   ·   v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ = Δxᵢ / Δt

The decoder outputs displacement in metres (scale sdisp = 0.02 m); next-step particle velocity is defined kinematically as Δxᵢ / Δt.

Implemented in examples/particle_nerd/transolver/model.py and examples/particle_nerd_batch/model.py.
06 / Particle Transolver: Active K Selection & Confining Boundary

Evaluate K active particles with M background anchors and a confining boundary

N = total bed particles · K = active particles near the rigid body · M = strided background anchors representing the (N − K) stationary bed.

1 · Unclamped 3D Priority Score

Rank all N particles by exact unclamped 3D signed distance dᵢ to the rigid body and speed ‖vᵢ‖; select the K lowest-score particles.

2 · Quadrature Anchors (M)

Append M strided background particles outside the active zone with multiplicity (N − K)/M_valid so slice sums match the full N-particle bed.

3 · Confining Boundary & Taper

Supply the stationary bed boundary (gap r_reach − dᵢ, upward-clamped normal n_cup, friction μ) and C¹ displacement taper γᵢ at the pocket edge.

Pipeline: Select K active + M anchor particles → evaluate Warp features on (K + M) only → run weighted Transolver → apply C¹ boundary taper γᵢ → scatter K displacements.
Budget K is set automatically from material friction and stiffness (f_auto = min(0.15, 0.032(1 + 1.5μ + 0.15Ê))) or specified via --active-k.
07 / Particle Transolver: Continuum Packing & 0.5 s Recurrent Training

Autoregressive 0.5 s training and minimum-spacing continuum packing

1 · End-to-End 0.5 s Recurrent Training

Settled start3 history frames after initial gravity settling
AutoregressiveFeed x̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ = xᵢⁿ + Δxᵢ back for 0.5 s
Full BPTTBackprop through all steps + Warp features
eᵢₜ = ‖(x̂ᵢₜ − xᵢₜMPM) / Δt‖²
Lbal = 0.5 meanmoving(eᵢₜ) + 0.5 meanstationary(eᵢₜ)
Ltotal = (1 − wc) Lbal + wc meandᵢ ≤ d_margin(eᵢₜ)
  • 0.5 s recurrent horizon: 25 steps at Δt = 20 ms (50 Hz) or 100 steps at Δt = 5 ms (200 Hz) with zero teacher forcing inside the window.
  • Balanced + contact-margin supervision: weights moving and stationary groups equally while sharpening accuracy within the rigid-body contact margin.
  • Pushforward curriculum: optional no-grad unroll prefix trains the network directly on its own multi-step particle distribution.

2 · Continuum Packing Incompressibility

δxᵢ = kpack Σdᵢⱼ < d_min (dmin − dᵢⱼ) · wup(zᵢ − zⱼ) · (x̂ᵢ − x̂ⱼ) / dᵢⱼ

Because point-cloud slice attention has no explicit local neighbor repulsion, particles at nearly identical coordinates receive nearly identical decoded displacements. Applying 2 differentiable repulsion iterations enforces minimum grain spacing d_min and floor non-penetration over multi-thousand-step rollouts.

Activation checkpointing keeps peak training memory under 0.9–1.8 GiB per GPU across 100 recurrent steps.

09 / Contact Force Estimation: Responsibilities & Surface Kinematics

Three contact responsibilities and surface velocity extrapolation

InteractionComponentState updated
Material ↔ material & boundariesParticle TransolverNext particle positions x̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ and velocities v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ = Δxᵢ / Δt
Material → rigid bodyAnalytical RFT contact solverMaterial-on-body force Fbody and COM torque Tbody
Rigid body ↔ rigid groundNewton collision + MJWarpRigid-body pose qⁿ⁺¹ and velocity Vgenn+1
1 · Velocity gradient Gᵢ

Fit 3×3 spatial velocity gradient Gᵢ from neighbors within 6.5 cm via Wendland C² weighted least squares:

v̂surface,in+1 = v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ + Gᵢ(sᵢ − xᵢⁿ)
2 · Candidate contacts

Query signed distance dᵢ, outward normal nᵢ (body → material), and closest surface point sᵢ:

dᵢ ≤ Δt max(nᵢ · (Vsurface,in+1,* − v̂surface,in+1), 0)

Includes active penetrations (dᵢ ≤ 0) and approaching contacts within Δt.

3 · Coupled impulse & wrench

Solve 6-DOF momentum balance with anisotropic RFT mobility and Coulomb friction; integrate wrench:

Fbody = Σᵢ jᵢ / Δt
Tbody = Σᵢ (sᵢ − cⁿ) × jᵢ / Δt
The estimator’s auxiliary material-velocity response is used only inside the contact solve; Transolver’s predicted particle states are never modified a second time.
All contact detection, gradient fitting, and impulse solves execute entirely on the GPU in Warp.
10 / Contact Force Estimation: Anisotropic RFT Mobility & Coulomb Law

Directional effective material mass and compliant Coulomb friction

1 · Orientation-Dependent Normal & Tangential Effective Mass

κn,i(nz,i) = κvert max(nz,i, 0)p + κhoriz(1 − nz,i²) + κup max(−nz,i, 0)
mn,ieff = κn,i(nz,i) mᵢ   ·   mt,ieff = κt mᵢ

Here nᵢ = (nx,i, ny,i, nz,i) is the unit normal from material toward the rigid body. Vertical compression against the confined bed (n_z,i > 0) mobilizes high bearing resistance (κ_vert = 100–220, p = 3), while lateral plowing (n_z,i ≈ 0, κ_horiz = 8) and tangential slip (κ_t = 4) yield plastically toward the free surface.

2 · Closed-Form Contact Impulse at Trial Body Velocity Vgenn+1,k

eᵢk = Jᵢ Vgenn+1,k − v̂surface,in+1   ·   γᵢ = [max(dᵢ, 0) + β min(dᵢ, 0)] / Δt
pᵢk = max(0, −mn,ieff(nᵢ · eᵢk + γᵢ))   ·   tᵢk,free = −mt,ieff [eᵢk − (nᵢ · eᵢk)nᵢ]
tᵢk = min(1, μ pᵢk / ‖tᵢk,free‖) tᵢk,free   ·   jᵢk = pᵢk nᵢ + tᵢk

At any trial rigid-body velocity Vgenn+1,k, each contact’s normal impulse pᵢk ≥ 0 and Coulomb-cone tangential impulse ‖tᵢk‖ ≤ μ pᵢk are exact closed-form functions of Vgenn+1,k.

Strictly passive analytical mechanics: guarantees unilateral non-adhesion (pᵢk ≥ 0) and Coulomb friction cone bounds on every substep.
11 / Contact Force Estimation: 6-DOF Body-Velocity Solve & Wrench

Solve 6 coupled momentum-balance equations per rigid body on the GPU

Algorithm · One Coupled Timestep (n → n+1)

InputParticle positions xᵢⁿ, velocities vᵢⁿ, history; rigid-body pose qⁿ, velocity Vgenn, mass matrix M(qⁿ), known loads τknown, Δt. OutputUpdated particle state (x̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹, v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹), material-on-body wrench (Fbody, Tbody), and next rigid-body state (qⁿ⁺¹, Vgenn+1).
1Predict particle motionRun Particle Transolver on active subset K + M anchors → next positions x̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ and velocities v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ = Δxᵢ / Δt.
2Free motion & contactsCompute free rigid-body velocity Vgenn+1,* and extrapolate material surface velocities v̂surface,in+1 = v̂ᵢⁿ⁺¹ + Gᵢ(sᵢ − xᵢⁿ).
3Solve 6-DOF rigid-body velocity Vgenn+1,k via damped Newton iterations (k = 0, 1, …):
AClosed-form impulsesEvaluate relative velocity eᵢk = Jᵢ Vgenn+1,k − v̂surface,in+1 and closed-form RFT impulse jᵢk(Vgenn+1,k) at all contacts in parallel.
B6×6 Newton stepAssemble 6-D momentum residual Rk and exact 6×6 Jacobian Dk; solve Dk δVk = −Rk with backtracking line search.
4Verify impulse residualConfirm local impulse residual < 10⁻⁹ with anisotropic compliance Kᵢ = Fᵢ M⁻¹ Fᵢᵀ + diag(1/mn,ieff, 1/mt,ieff, 1/mt,ieff).
5Integrate rigid bodySum Fbody = Σᵢ jᵢ / Δt and Tbody = Σᵢ (rᵢ × jᵢ) / Δt; advance rigid body in Newton MJWarp.

Efficiency: Instead of iterating over Nc coupled 3D impulse variables, the Newton solver iterates over only 6 shared rigid-body velocity unknowns, converging to 10⁻¹² in 5–7 iterations (~0.35 ms on GPU).

1. Free rigid-body velocity & surface kinematics

Vgenn+1,*Free 6-DOF rigid-body velocity (linear + angular) after gravity, actuation, and known external loads, before material contact. Jᵢ(qⁿ)3 × 6 contact point Jacobian mapping body COM twist Vgen to world velocity at surface point sᵢ: Jᵢ Vgen = VCOM + ω × (sᵢ − cⁿ). eᵢkRelative surface velocity at trial body velocity Vgenn+1,k.
Vgenn+1,* = Vgenn + Δt M(qⁿ)⁻¹ [τknown − b(qⁿ, Vgenn)]
eᵢk = Jᵢ Vgenn+1,k − v̂surface,in+1
For an articulated robot foot proxy, Vgenn+1,* is captured directly from the rigid solver after rewinding the previous step’s lagged proxy wrench.

2. Exact 3×3 local contact Jacobian ∂jᵢk / ∂eᵢk

Sticking regime (‖tᵢk,free‖ ≤ μ pᵢk)Normal and tangential responses are decoupled diagonal scalars (−mn,ieff, −mt,ieff, −mt,ieff). Sliding regime (‖tᵢk,free‖ > μ pᵢk)Tangential impulse projects onto the Coulomb circle of radius r = μ pᵢk, coupling normal compression to friction magnitude.
∂pᵢk / ∂en,ik = −mn,ieff   ·   ∂tᵢk / ∂en,ik = −μ mn,ieff (tᵢk,free / ‖tᵢk,free‖)
∂tᵢk / ∂et,ik = −mt,ieff (r / ‖tᵢk,free‖) (I₂ − t̂ t̂ᵀ)
Computed analytically per contact thread in float64 inside force_estimation/body_velocity.py.

3. Solve 6 coupled momentum-balance equations

Rk ∈ ℝ⁶Mismatch between trial velocity Vgenn+1,k and the velocity produced by all contact impulses jᵢk(Vgenn+1,k). Dk ∈ ℝ6×6Exact derivative ∂Rk/∂Vgenn+1,k accumulated in parallel across 128 threads via shared-memory tile reductions.
Rk = Vgenn+1,k − Vgenn+1,* − M⁻¹ Σᵢ Jᵢᵀ jᵢk
Dk = I₆ − M⁻¹ Σᵢ Jᵢᵀ (∂jᵢk / ∂eᵢk) Jᵢ
Dk δVk = −Rk   ·   Vgenn+1,k+1 = Vgenn+1,k + ηk δVk
Solved in-kernel by 6×6 Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting and monotone residual line search ηk ∈ {1, ½, ¼, …}.

4. Verify impulse fixed point & integrate rigid-body wrench

Kᵢ ∈ ℝ3×3Per-contact compliance matrix combining rigid-body point mobility and anisotropic material slack. Fbody; TbodyTotal material force and torque about the rigid-body COM cⁿ, applied to MJWarp over Δt.
Kᵢ = Fᵢ M⁻¹ Fᵢᵀ + diag(1 / mn,ieff, 1 / mt,ieff, 1 / mt,ieff)
Fbody = Σᵢ jᵢ / Δt   ·   Tbody = Σᵢ ((sᵢ − cⁿ) × jᵢ) / Δt
Because Kᵢ uses the exact same (mn,ieff, mt,ieff) as the 6-DOF Newton solve, verification finishes in 1 iteration at 10⁻⁹ tolerance.

16 / Implementation: Architecture Summary

Current production architecture across Shovel and G1

ComponentCurrent implementation
Particle Transolver4-layer, 4-head, 64-slice transformer (hidden = 128) embedding 25 particle features + 8 collider features per body. Trained recurrently over 0.5 s horizons (25 steps at 50 Hz for Shovel; 100 steps at 200 Hz for G1).
Active K + AnchorsUnclamped 3D signed distance + speed ranking selects K active particles (1,408 Shovel; 5,312 G1) + M quadrature-weighted background anchors (64 Shovel; 512 G1) with upward-clamped confining cup boundary in floor_slot.
Continuum Packing2-iteration differentiable B-spline pair repulsion (d_min = 16.5 mm) + rigid floor non-penetration (z ≥ −0.29 m) prevents point-cloud coalescence over multi-second rollouts.
Analytical Contact Solver6-DOF Newton body-velocity solver with Granular RFT directional effective mass (κ_vert, κ_horiz, κ_up) and decoupled tangential slip compliance (κ_t), verified to 10⁻⁹ impulse residual. No neural force head.
Rigid-Body CouplingNewton MJWarp integrates rigid-body and articulated robot dynamics at 200 Hz with direct rigid-ground collision detection.

All simulation, training, and batched inference code lives in examples/particle_nerd and examples/particle_nerd_batch.
