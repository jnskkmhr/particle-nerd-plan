What data trained the selected particle model?

The G1-sized scene is out of distribution. It changes the sand-bed dimensions, body geometry, and—at 0.04 m—particle spacing. Mouse interaction and long play sessions introduce further changes. This confirms OOD inputs; it does not prove OOD is the only source of bad predictions.

Which checkpoint?

selected_model.pt is unroll4/best.pt, update 9,000 of four-step rollout training. Its embedded episode list names 48 training and 6 validation episodes. Its initializer is the long one-step model at update 12,000, which lists the same 48 training episodes. The later collection contains 156 training episodes, but that larger count does not describe this checkpoint.

Fixed geometry and particle sampling

  • Sand bed: 0.32 × 0.32 × 0.16 m. Initial sand surface at z = 0; rigid supporting floor at z = −0.16 m.
  • 4,000 particles on a 20 × 20 × 10 cell-centered lattice, without spatial jitter. Spacing 0.016 m; radius 0.008 m.
  • MPM reference grid: 0.02 m, at most 25 iterations, timestep 0.005 s. Each episode has 300 transitions (1.5 seconds).
  • Box: 0.12 × 0.12 × 0.08 m. Cylinder: diameter 0.12 m, height 0.08 m. Sphere: diameter 0.12 m. Sizes are fixed within each shape family.
  • Five fixed rigid bodies form the floor and four walls; one freely dynamic body interacts with the particles. The MJWarp–MPM solver supplies two-way coupling.

Each combination has three shapes and four episodes per shape: 4 × 3 × 4 = 48. These are correlated combinations, not independent samples over density, friction, modulus, and mass. Poisson ratio is 0.3; yield pressure is 10¹⁵ Pa; tensile yield ratio, yield stress, hardening, dilatancy, and viscosity are zero. Body friction is 0.6.

Initial states and motion coverage

Initial body-center x/y offsets are uniform within ±0.025 m. The configured z offset 0.05–0.12 m is added to the 0.08 m nominal center, giving center heights 0.13–0.20 m above the initial sand surface. Roll/pitch offsets are within ±0.35 rad; yaw spans approximately ±π.

Launch velocities are fixed per group and shape: vx = +0.12 m/s for groups 1 and 3, −0.12 m/s for groups 2 and 4; vy = +0.04 m/s for boxes/cylinders and −0.04 m/s for spheres; vz = −0.1 m/s. World angular velocities are (0,0,0), (3,0,2), (0,−3,2), and (0,0,0) rad/s for groups 1–4. No force or trajectory is prescribed after initialization. There are no mouse-driven trajectories.

Validation is a held-out material combination

The six validation episodes use density 1,200 kg/m³, friction 0.4, modulus 10⁶ Pa, and body mass 1.5 kg: two episodes per shape. Geometry and sampling match training, but this exact material/mass combination is not a training row. These are the default reset scenes in play.

What changed in G1 play?

A 2 × 2 × 0.3 m bed replaces the small bed. At 0.04 m sampling, there are 39,690 particles; at 0.02 m, 296,875 particles. The box becomes the G1 foot collision size, 0.17 × 0.06 × 0.01 m. Its mass remains 1.5 kg; its homogeneous-box inertia is recomputed. The default validation material is retained. The initial box is level and stationary with center z = 0.10 m. No retraining was done for these changes.

The GNN's neighbor radius stays 0.035 m. On the initial regular lattice, changing sampling from 0.016 m to about 0.032 m reduces an interior particle's neighbors from 32 to 6. At 0.02 m, spacing is close to training, but bed depth, distance to walls, foot geometry, and interaction remain different.

Play commands

# Default: recorded validation geometry, G1 colors only
./play.sh --viewer rtx

# Explicit OOD scene; all particles still updated
./play.sh --viewer rtx --scene g1-testbed --grid-size 0.04
./play.sh --viewer rtx --scene g1-testbed --grid-size 0.02

Restart an already-running play process to load the corrected default. Reliable G1 deployment requires data covering the target bed, sampling, foot geometry, and applied forces, followed by separate rollout validation. The existing small-scene timing and accuracy results do not apply to the G1 scene.
